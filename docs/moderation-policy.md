# Moderation policy

The registry favors traceable evidence over entry count.

- Discovery leads remain in `candidates/` until they meet the stable threshold.
- Maintainers reject misleading official claims, fabricated verification, unclear licenses, duplicate identities and secret-bearing submissions.
- `manifest_checked` and `source_reviewed` are static evidence labels, not security endorsements.
- Material changes retain the Git history and fixed commit that supported the previous conclusion.
- Sponsored or paid relationships must be disclosed and cannot change evidence grades or risk labels.
- A removed, archived or incompatible plugin may be marked deprecated rather than erased when history is useful.

Corrections should identify the exact field, a stable evidence URL and the proposed replacement. Disputes are resolved from primary sources whenever possible.
